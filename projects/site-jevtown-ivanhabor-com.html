--- layout: "resource" title: "Jevtown | Awesome Jev" description: "Town of 10,000 computed personas that reads a post, listing, product, or headline: one opening request scores the text against about 60 audience attributes, 83 for a listing or a product, plus seven moderation questions, and plans the first wave of 600 readers; batched Choice questions then return each persona's reaction, and the text reaches the next wave only while glad reactions outweigh sorry ones. Also returns the audience by interest, job, age, city, and budget, the question buyers would ask a listing first, and a demand curve over an author-set price ladder.
